# Arreat Systems

**Less work. More focus on what matters.**

Arreat Systems SIA is a Latvian software company, founded in 2026 by Arnis Kemlers. We build SaaS platforms and the integrations around them: the pipes that connect your bank, your e-invoicing, your payment providers and your mail to your business, so your people stop copy-pasting between systems and start doing the work that actually matters.

## What we do

Two things, done deeply:

- **SaaS platforms.** Multi-tenant web applications with real authorization boundaries: per-tenant data isolation, role bundles, two-factor sign-in, field-level audit logs, GDPR export and anonymisation. Built so tenant one can never see tenant two.
- **Integrations.** The boring parts of business, automated: bank statement parsing, EN 16931 e-invoices (UBL 2.1, CII), payment initiation, email intake pipelines, AI document extraction with an EU residency option. Every integration ships with a fallback, and refuses to guess when it cannot be sure.

The promise is the same for both: automate the repetitive, audit the rest, and give the business back its evenings. Integrations self-audit: a bank statement is re-checked against its own running balance before a single line is accepted, and no document is ever booked by a machine without a human confirming it.

## Proof of capacity

Our flagship build, **MyHouse**, is building-maintenance accounting: a double entry ledger with integer cents, a rule driven billing engine, and a resident portal, for houses that manage themselves. We mention it not to sell it, but because it shows the depth we bring to a contract: if we can keep a building's books balanced to the cent, we can integrate yours.
